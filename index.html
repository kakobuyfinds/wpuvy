Reading room / Three practical articles

Make the next click count.

CSSBuy finds, QC photos, sizing and shipping are useful research topics when they answer a concrete buying question.

01 / CSSBuy QC photos

Look beyond the first image

Start with the selected size and colour. Compare front, back and detail views. Look for visible marks, missing pieces and mismatched variations. Ask for measurements when fit is unclear.

Photos cannot confirm authenticity, durability or comfort. A clean image is a starting point for inspection, not a quality guarantee.

  • Check both shoes and the size labels.
  • Compare garment seams and fastenings.
  • Resolve unclear details before shipment.
02 / CSSBuy sizing guide

A fit-first approach to clothing links

Measure a garment you already like and compare it with the listing chart. Record the unit and measurement method. Letter sizes are not consistent between sellers.

For tops, compare chest width, body length and shoulder width. For trousers, compare waist, rise and inseam. Stretch, lining and a relaxed cut can all change how a piece feels.

For sets, check each component separately; one size label does not guarantee that both pieces suit your proportions.

03 / CSSBuy shipping guide

Plan the parcel before choosing a route

Keep the product amount separate from domestic delivery, agent charges, international freight and destination taxes. Estimates are not final invoices.

Compare routes for your destination and item type. Packed dimensions and chargeable weight can change the quote. Shoe boxes and bulky outerwear deserve particular attention.

For US, UK and EU deliveries, check the rules that apply to your exact destination. Do not assume another buyer's delivery time or tax treatment applies to you.

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a reference list of product links used for shopping research. It may include categories, pictures, sizing notes and QC references. Each original listing still needs to be checked before ordering.

Does this page provide an official CSSBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide, not an official CSSBuy service or a downloadable inventory file. Its category buttons open hipobuyfind.com; they do not open a CSSBuy checkout.

Can QC photos prove a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help reveal visible condition and variation details, but they do not establish authenticity. For branded goods, check seller authorization, provenance and the applicable return policy.

How should I choose a size?

Compare the listing measurements with an item that fits you. Check the units, measurement method and selected variation. Ask for clarification when the chart is incomplete instead of relying only on S, M or L.

Is a listed item price the full delivered cost?

Usually it is only one part of the cost. Check domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion and destination taxes or duties before paying.

What should I do with a broken product link?

Search using the exact model or item description and check the replacement seller listing carefully. Do not assume a similar image means the same materials, sizing or product.
